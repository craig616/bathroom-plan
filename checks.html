Pick one: the panel right of the sink

Buying the extra panel gets you Plan. It's 2200 long: floor to the top of the tall cupboard, not to the ceiling. Above that, the 62×80 you already have fills the last 400 (panel 7).

Plan and Your idea cost the same and need the same purchase. The only difference is a 12 mm beige strip. Plan runs it from floor to ceiling between the sink and tall cupboard. Your idea shows it only above the worktop and moves everything left of the tall cupboard 12 mm.

Go with Plan. It's IKEA's own layout and the model is already drawn that way, so nothing moves.

The one job in the room

Find the studs. You said the wall is hollow and you can't see any glue blobs behind the board. That means plasterboard on studs, so the cupboards must screw into the studs.

Run the stud finder across the whole back wall at the height of the tops of the wall cupboards, about 2.6 m up. Pencil a mark at each stud. If it beeps for a cable anywhere, mark that too.
